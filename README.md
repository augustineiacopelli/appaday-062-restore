**Live:** https://augustineiacopelli.github.io/appaday-062-restore/

## What it does

Restore is a guided stretching and prayer app for cooling down after a workout or quieting the mind before rest. Pick one of three routines, Post-Workout Cooldown, Evening Calm, or Quick Reset, or hold any single stretch from the full library on its own. Each stretch pairs a silhouette pose illustration with plain-language form cues, a breathing halo that expands on a four-second inhale and settles on a six-second exhale, a scripture verse, and a short breath prayer. Every session closes with the Glory Be.

## Features

- Thirteen stretches across mobility areas: hamstrings, hips, spine, shoulders, neck, and full-body recovery
- Silhouette pictogram illustrations for every pose, built as inline SVG
- Three guided routines plus a browsable stretch library for standalone holds
- Animated breathing halo and countdown ring synced to each hold
- Bilateral stretches cue a "Switch sides" chime and flash at the halfway mark
- Scripture verse and short breath prayer tied to every stretch
- Local progress tracking: day streak, total sessions, total minutes, and a 28-day dot calendar
- Soft WebAudio chimes on transitions, toggleable from the header
- Reduced-motion support for the breathing animation

## Tech

Single-file HTML, CSS, and vanilla JavaScript. No build step, no external dependencies beyond Google Fonts. All progress data is stored in the browser's localStorage under a dedicated key and never leaves the device.
